---
title: "Outgoing Webhook"
description: "The next retry of an invoice, sent to the profile's webhook URL"
---

On a profile whose `revenue_recovery_trigger_model` is `external_triggers`, you trigger the retries of an invoice and Hyperswitch tells you when. After it records a transaction you sent to [`POST /v2/recovery`](/v2/revenue-recovery/revenue-recovery--create), it decides the invoice's next retry and sends it to the profile's webhook URL.

| Event | Sent when | `schedule_time` | `reason` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `recovery_retry_scheduled` | A retry is scheduled | When to trigger the retry | `null` |
| `recovery_limits_exhausted` | A limit of the profile stops the retries | `null` | `retries_exhausted`, `grace_period_expired` or `hard_declined` |

A transaction that doesn't change the next retry sends no webhook: for example, one made with the customer present, or a charge that recovers the invoice.

Each webhook has its own `event_id`, and the request is signed in the `X-Webhook-Signature-512` header, like every Hyperswitch outgoing webhook.

## Webhook body

<ResponseField name="merchant_id" type="string" required>
  The provider merchant id (platform/business owner).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="event_id" type="string" required>
  The unique event id for each webhook.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="event_type" type="string" required>
  `recovery_retry_scheduled` or `recovery_limits_exhausted`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="content" type="object" required>
  <Expandable title="properties" defaultOpen>
    <ResponseField name="type" type="string" required>
      Always `recovery_retry_details`.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="object" type="object" required>
      The next retry of the invoice. Every field is always present.

      <Expandable title="properties" defaultOpen>
        <ResponseField name="id" type="string" required>
          Unique identifier of the invoice in Hyperswitch.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="merchant_reference_id" type="string" required>
          Unique identifier of the invoice in the merchant's billing system.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="retry_count" type="integer" required>
          Number of the next retry, counted the way the `max_retry_count` of the profile counts them: the first retry after the original payment is 1.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="schedule_time" type="string | null" required>
          The time to trigger the retry at, then report its result through the recovery API. `null` when no retry is scheduled.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="reason" type="string | null" required>
          Why no retry is scheduled: `retries_exhausted`, `grace_period_expired` or `hard_declined`. `null` when a retry is scheduled.
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="timestamp" type="string">
  The time at which the webhook was sent.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="processor_merchant_id" type="string | null">
  The merchant id of the merchant account whose connector credentials are used for payment processing.
</ResponseField>
